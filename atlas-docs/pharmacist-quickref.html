Adherence Patterns
INA · Intentional
Patient chooses not to take medication. Side effects, doubts, cost. Needs motivational counseling — reminders alone will not work.
UNA · Unintentional
Patient intends to take but forgets or has access barriers. Pill organiser, alarm, blister pack. Most common pattern.
Mixed Pattern
Both INA and UNA present. Often polypharmacy. Address barriers first, then motivation.
High Adherence
Fully adherent. Positive reinforcement. Document for clinical record. Follow-up 30 days.
Sentinel Alerts — When they fire
!
Urgent (red) — INA pattern, score <4, or ZOE clinical flag. Act within 7 days.
!
Moderate (amber) — Medium adherence. Follow-up in 14 days.
✓
Routine (green) — High adherence. Scheduled 30-day reassessment.

Sentinel alerts appear in the right sidebar of your dashboard. Click "Mark Reviewed" when actioned to clear the queue.

Follow-Up Link

After every ZOE session, click "📋 Copy reminder link" on the follow-up card. Paste into SMS, email, or patient portal. The link opens a pre-filled ATLAS assessment for that patient.

MTM Billing — CMS CPT Codes
CodeWhen to useTime
99605 Initial comprehensive MTM encounter — first time seeing this patient for adherence. ATLAS auto-assigns on first log. First 15 min
99606 Follow-up targeted MTM — patient seen before. ATLAS auto-assigns after first encounter. First 15 min

Auto-assignment logic ATLAS automatically suggests the correct CPT code in the MTM Audit Log based on whether this is a first or follow-up encounter and the adherence pattern. Always verify against your payer's current requirements before submission.

MAP PE Domain Analysis

Available Researcher tier+. Every MAP result shows a PE Domain Profile — composite score + three domain bars. Formula: PE = (A × E × C)1/3.

DomainSource questionsWhat it measures
A · ArchitectureQ2, Q3, Q6Belief, motivation, intentional skipping
E · ExecutionQ1, Q4, Q5, Q8Habitual reliability, everyday behaviour
C · ContextQ7Perceived regimen burden and friction
Constraint label on result screen After every MMAS-8 submission, the result modal shows which domain is lowest: Architecture-constrained → motivational counseling; Execution-constrained → habit systems; Context-constrained → regimen simplification. CSV export adds mmas_pe · mmas_a · mmas_e · mmas_c columns automatically.
PEACS — Predictive Emergence

PE = (BASE × MVMT × STRATA)1/3. Scores range 0–1. Above 0.70 predicts sustained adherence.

DimensionWhat it measuresWindow
BASE Foundation — how consistently patient follows regimen under normal conditions 30 days
MVMT Flexibility — adherence maintained across disruptions (travel, illness, schedule changes) 7 days
STRATA Social determinants — housing, transport, support, health literacy, treatment beliefs 90 days
PE ZoneScoreClinical meaning
Optimal≥ 0.85High predicted stability — routine follow-up
Good0.70–0.84Generally stable — light monitoring
Moderate0.55–0.69Instability risk — targeted intervention
Poor< 0.55High risk — priority intervention, check STRATA

Troubleshooting
Cannot reach validation server Check internet connection. ATLAS requires active connection. Try mobile data hotspot.
ZOE not responding to voice Microphone permission must be allowed in Chrome. Check browser settings → Privacy → Microphone. ZOE does not work in Safari or Firefox.
Patient submitted under wrong workspace Contact info@adherence.cc with your key prefix and the approximate timestamp. Records can be moved between workspaces.
Export shows zero records Ensure you are logged in with your workspace key (green chip top-left shows your key). Explorer mode does not allow export.
