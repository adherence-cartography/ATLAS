Before you begin

Please read the following information about this assessment. Your participation is voluntary and entirely anonymous.

Purpose · What this study collects
Why we ask
The ATLAS platform is a global, community-based research infrastructure measuring medication adherence using the MMAS-8 (Morisky Medication Adherence Scale) developed by Philip Morisky. Your responses contribute to real-time population health mapping across more than 150 countries. No names, email addresses, or identifying personal information are collected at any point.
Voluntary Participation · Your rights
You are in control
Participation is entirely voluntary. You may stop at any time without consequence. Submitting your answers constitutes your agreement to have them included in the anonymous global dataset for research purposes. You have the right to withdraw, and because data is anonymized upon submission, individual records cannot be retroactively removed.
Data Privacy · Anonymization
What we store
Your responses are stored anonymously. We record: your MAP composite score and domain subscores (Architectural, Executive, and Contextual adherence), your approximate geographic location (country and city level only), a random session ID (not linked to you), and a timestamp. No personally identifiable information is collected or retained.
Geolocation · Map contribution
Placing you on the map
To show your contribution on the live global map, we use your approximate location — country and city level only. No street address or precise coordinates are ever stored. Your browser may prompt for permission; if you decline, we fall back to an IP-based estimate that achieves the same city-level result.
📍 By proceeding, you agree to city-level location being recorded alongside your anonymous responses.

CMS Documentation Checklist — CCM (per patient per month)
Only one clinician per patient per month may bill CCM. Cannot be billed same month as PCM (99424-99427). Pharmacists bill as supervised staff under QHCP; NPs/PAs/MDs bill 99491 directly.
📡 Remote Therapeutic Monitoring — CMS
RTM: Medication Adherence Data · 16+ Days Per Month
Medication adherence monitoring qualifies for RTM (unlike RPM, which requires physiologic data). Requires ≥16 days of adherence data per 30-day period. Platform data collection must meet FDA Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) criteria — consult legal/regulatory counsel for classification.
98975 — Setup

RTM Management Add-on

RPM ≠ RTM: Do not use RPM codes (99453–99458) for medication adherence — those require physiologic data. RTM (98975–98981) is the correct pathway. · 2025 conversion factor: $32.35

Contact patient within 2 business days of discharge. Medication reconciliation is required (not optional) and must be documented by the date of the face-to-face visit. 30-day billing window from discharge date.

Before you continue

About the Publication License

📋
What it covers

A Publication License grants you the right to cite MAP, MMAS-8, and/or PEACS data collected through ATLAS in a published work — thesis, dissertation, peer-reviewed article, or conference paper.

⚖️
Why it is required

MAP and PEACS are proprietary validated instruments. Using data collected with licensed instruments in a publication requires formal permission — this license provides that permission and protects the integrity of the citation record.

✅
What you receive

A permanent, non-expiring license key (PUB-XXXX-XXXX), a signed Letter of Permission (PDF), and a citation certificate for your IRB file or appendix. The license is tied to your participant count at the time of issuance.
