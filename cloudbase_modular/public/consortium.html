The MAP Instrument · What We Measure
Multidimensional Adherence Parameters

MAP is an 8-item triadic adherence instrument validated across chronic-disease populations. Unlike single-scale tools, MAP decomposes adherence into three independent behavioral domains, producing a composite PE score and a PEACS phenotype classification. Pharmacists can administer it at the counter in under 3 minutes.
