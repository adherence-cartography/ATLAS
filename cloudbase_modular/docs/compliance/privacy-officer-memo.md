# Privacy Officer Designation — Adherence Inc.

## Designation

Pursuant to the requirements of the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), 45 CFR §164.530(a), Adherence Inc. hereby formally designates:

**Privacy Officer**
Email: compliance@adherence.cc
Organization: Adherence Inc.
Platform: ATLAS — Adherence Cartography (atlas.adherence.cc)

## Responsibilities

The Privacy Officer is responsible for:

1. **Policy Development & Maintenance** — Developing, implementing, and maintaining privacy policies and procedures in accordance with HIPAA Privacy Rule (45 CFR Part 164, Subpart E) and applicable state and international privacy laws.

2. **Training Coordination** — Ensuring all workforce members receive HIPAA privacy training at onboarding and annually thereafter. Maintaining training completion records for a minimum of 6 years.

3. **Breach Management** — Receiving and investigating reports of suspected privacy breaches. Coordinating breach notifications to affected individuals (within 60 days of discovery) and to HHS (annually for breaches affecting <500 individuals; within 60 days for breaches affecting ≥500 individuals).

4. **Business Associate Oversight** — Maintaining a current inventory of Business Associates, ensuring executed BAAs are on file prior to any PHI disclosure, and reviewing BAA compliance annually.

5. **Data Subject Requests** — Receiving and processing Data Subject Access Requests (DSAR) within the timeframes specified by applicable law (HIPAA: 30 days; GDPR: 30 days; UAE PDPL: 30 days).

6. **Regulatory Liaison** — Serving as primary contact for HHS Office for Civil Rights (OCR), state attorneys general, and international data protection authorities in matters relating to ATLAS platform privacy compliance.

7. **Audit Coordination** — Facilitating privacy-related audits, including review of audit trail records maintained in the ATLAS platform.

This designation memo shall be retained for a minimum of 6 years from the date of creation or the date it was last in effect, whichever is later (45 CFR §164.530(j)).
