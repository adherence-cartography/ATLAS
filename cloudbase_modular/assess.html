Before you begin

Please review the following. Your participation is voluntary and completely anonymous.

📋
Purpose · What this study collects
Why we ask
Medication non-adherence is the single largest preventable cause of treatment failure worldwide — yet it remains invisible in most health systems. ATLAS maps adherence behavior in real time across every country using the MAP (Multidimensional Adherence Parameters) instrument. Your response becomes a data point on that map. In return, you receive an immediate personal adherence profile with actionable guidance. No names, emails, or personal identifiers are ever collected.
✋
Voluntary Participation · Your rights
You are in control
Participation is entirely voluntary. You may stop at any time. Submitting your answers consents to their inclusion in the anonymous global dataset. Because responses are fully anonymized the moment you submit, they cannot be linked back to you or individually removed.
🛡️
Data Privacy · Anonymization
What we store
We store: your MAP composite score and domain subscores (Architectural, Executive, and Contextual adherence), approximate location (country and city from browser or IP), a random session ID not linked to you, and a timestamp. No personally identifiable information is ever collected or retained.
📍
Geolocation · Map contribution
Placing you on the map
We use city-level location only — no street address or precise coordinates are stored. If you decline browser permission we use IP-based city estimation.
📍 By proceeding, you agree to city-level location being recorded alongside your anonymous responses.
