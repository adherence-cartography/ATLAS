About this Registry
What is an ATLAS Certificate?
Every Letter of Permission and Publication License issued by Adherence Cartography carries a unique key. Standard letters use the format MAP-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX; publication licenses use PUB-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX. Both are cryptographically generated server-side and permanently registered in this database.
Who should use this?
IRB officers, ethics committee members, journal editors, and peer reviewers who have received a Letter of Permission and want to independently confirm it is genuine and currently active before accepting it as documentation.
Suspected Forgery?
If a certificate number returns "not found" or the details do not match the letter you received, contact info@adherence.cc immediately with the certificate number and a copy of the letter.
MAP Instrument Licensing
The MAP (Medication Adherence Profile) is an independently developed medication adherence assessment authored by Adherence Cartography (Adherence Inc.). Use in research or clinical settings requires a valid, active Letter of Permission issued through the ATLAS platform.
